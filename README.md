# 🎵 STrymd — Apple Music for Android

Experience the elegance of Apple Music on your Android phone. STrymd brings the iconic Apple Music interface, smooth animations, and premium listening experience directly to your Android device.

**Finally, the iOS music app experience Android deserves.**

## ✨ What Makes STrymd Special

### Beautiful, Familiar Design
- **Apple Music-inspired UI** — If you've loved Apple Music on iOS, you'll feel right at home. We've meticulously matched the look, feel, and navigation of Apple's iconic music player.
- **Liquid Glass Design** — Stunning glassmorphism effects that adapt to your album artwork with dynamic colors. Every album feels premium.
- **Smooth Animations** — Silky-smooth transitions and interactions that make browsing your music library feel effortless.

### Music at Your Fingertips
- **Local Music Library** — Play all the music stored on your device with full control and beautiful visualization.
- **Smart Organization** — Browse by Artists, Albums, Playlists, and Songs. Your music library has never looked better.
- **Search Anywhere** — Quickly find your favorite tracks with instant search across your entire library.
- **Dark Mode** — Eye-friendly dark interface that looks stunning and saves battery on OLED screens.

### Premium Features
- **JioSaavn Integration** *(Coming Soon)* — Stream directly from JioSaavn's massive catalog of Indian music and beyond.
- **Spotify Integration** *(Coming Soon)* — For Spotify Premium users, seamless access to your saved tracks and playlists.
- **YouTube Music Integration** *(Coming Soon)* — Access music videos and audio tracks from YouTube's vast library.

### Uninterrupted Listening
- **Background Playback** — Keep your music playing while using other apps or with the screen off.
- **Notification Controls** — Pause, skip, or play directly from your notifications.
- **Persistent Queue** — Your playback continues exactly where you left off, even after closing the app.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Install STrymd

1. **Download & Install**
   - Clone this repository or download the APK
   - Install on your Android device (API 26+)

2. **Grant Permissions**
   - STrymd needs permission to access your device's audio files
   - Tap **"Allow"** when prompted for media permissions

3. **Import Your Music**
   - Your entire device music library appears automatically
   - Organize by scanning for local audio files

4. **Start Listening**
   - Browse your library, tap a song, and enjoy
   - Swipe, tap, and interact just like you would on Apple Music
